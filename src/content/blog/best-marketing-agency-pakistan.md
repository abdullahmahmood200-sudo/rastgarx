Most businesses search for the best marketing agency in Pakistan and end up comparing logos, follower counts and monthly fees. Those are easy to compare, and they say very little about whether an agency can bring you customers.

Rastup is a marketing agency too, so keep that in mind as you read. The checks below work for any agency, including us.

## What makes a marketing agency good?

A good marketing agency connects its work to your revenue. It can explain who your customer is, why they should choose you, where to reach them and what happens after they click.

Look for these five signs:

- **Relevant proof.** The agency can show work for a business like yours, or explain clearly why its experience transfers.
- **Honest reporting.** Reports focus on enquiries, bookings and sales. Likes and reach are supporting numbers.
- **Clear ownership.** Your website, domain, ad accounts, pixels and analytics stay in your business's name.
- **Named people.** You know who will run your account and how much of their time you get.
- **Written terms.** Scope, price, timeline, revisions and notice period are all in the proposal.

## Start with the problem you need solved

"We need marketing" is too broad to hire against. Write down one goal before you speak to anyone, for example "get more consultation bookings for our clinic" or "sell more from our online store". Then match the goal to the type of agency.

| Your main problem | Type of agency to look for | What to ask to see |
|---|---|---|
| Plenty of visitors, too few enquiries | Web design and conversion agency | Sites built around one clear action |
| People do not know you exist | Advertising or creative agency | Ad concepts and campaigns in your industry |
| You need leads quickly | Performance marketing agency | How they track cost per lead or sale |
| You are invisible on Google | SEO agency | Rankings and leads earned for past clients |
| Your social pages are inactive | Social media marketing agency | Content that led to enquiries |
| Website, ads and follow-up are all disconnected | Full-service marketing agency | One plan covering the whole funnel |

## How to compare marketing agencies in Pakistan

### 1. Shortlist three agencies

Search for terms like "digital marketing agency in Pakistan" or "marketing company in Islamabad", then ask other business owners who they use. Check each agency's own website first. If it is slow, unclear or hard to use on a phone, expect similar work for you.

### 2. Review the work closely

Open the live websites and look at the actual ads. Check how they work on your phone. Then ask what the work achieved and how the agency measured it.

### 3. Speak to a past client

Ask for one client you can contact. Ask how the agency communicated, what went wrong and how it was handled. A client's account of a difficult month tells you more than a polished testimonial.

### 4. Compare written proposals

Put the proposals side by side. A lower monthly fee can cover far less work, so compare what is delivered, how often, and by whom. Ad spend should be listed separately from the agency's fee.

### 5. Start small if you can

A defined first project or a short trial shows you how the agency actually works before you commit to a longer relationship.

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. Which businesses like mine have you worked with?
2. What will you measure, and how often will I see it?
3. Who will work on my account each week?
4. Will the ad accounts, website, domain and analytics be in my business's name?
5. What exactly is included in the fee, and what costs extra?
6. Is ad spend paid directly by me?
7. How much notice do I need to give to stop?
8. What will you need from me to make this work?
9. What would make you advise me not to spend money on this?

The last question is useful. A good agency will be able to name situations where marketing is the wrong next step, such as a weak offer or a website that cannot convert the traffic.

## Red flags to avoid

- A guaranteed first-place ranking on Google. No agency controls Google's results.
- Guaranteed sales figures before anyone has looked at your business.
- Bought followers or engagement presented as growth.
- Ad accounts or your domain registered in the agency's name.
- Reports that contain only impressions, reach and likes.
- A long lock-in contract with no exit terms.
- Identical packages for every industry.

## Marketing agency, freelancer or in-house team?

| Option | Works best when | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Full-service marketing agency | You want one team responsible for the whole system | Check who actually does the work |
| Freelancer | You have one well-defined task | You manage and coordinate everything yourself |
| In-house team | Marketing is constant and you can train and manage people | Salaries, tools and a narrower range of skills |

## Does the agency need to be in your city?

Often it does not. Most marketing work happens by video call, WhatsApp and shared dashboards, so experience in your industry usually matters more than the agency's address.

If you prefer someone local, compare a [marketing agency in Islamabad](/locations/islamabad), [Lahore](/locations/lahore) or [Karachi](/locations/karachi) using the same checks. A local partner is most useful when the work involves on-site shoots, events or outdoor advertising.

## Why your website has to come first

Ads and SEO send people to your website, so a slow or confusing site wastes the money spent reaching them. Before you increase your budget, check that a visitor on a phone can understand what you do and take the next step within a few seconds.
