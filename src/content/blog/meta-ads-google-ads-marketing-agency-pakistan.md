Most ecommerce owners in Pakistan meet paid marketing through two platforms: Meta Ads, which covers Facebook and Instagram, and Google Ads. Both can bring customers to your store. Both can also spend your budget quickly when the setup, the offer or the numbers behind them are weak.

This guide explains what each platform is good at, how to tell whether your ads are actually profitable, and what to look for in a Meta Ads marketing agency or Google Ads partner. ## What is performance marketing?

Performance marketing is marketing judged on measurable results: orders, revenue, cost per customer and profit. Each campaign has a target, the tracking to measure it, and a rule for when to spend more.

For an online store, that means looking past clicks, likes and reach. The questions that matter are simpler:

- How many orders did the ads bring in?
- What did each order cost in ad spend?
- What was left after product, delivery, COD charges, returns and ads?

## What Meta Ads do for an ecommerce store

Meta Ads place your products in the Facebook and Instagram feeds, Stories, Reels and Messenger of people Meta predicts are likely to buy. People are scrolling, so the ad has to earn their attention in the first second.

Meta Ads tend to work well for:

- **Products people buy on impulse or by look:** clothing, shoes, beauty, jewellery, home decor and gifts.
- **New products** that people are unlikely to search for yet.
- **Retargeting** visitors who viewed a product or added it to the cart.
- **Video-led selling,** where a short reel can show the product in use.

What decides results on Meta:

1. **Creative.** The video or image does most of the targeting today. Test new angles every week.
2. **The offer.** Price, delivery promise, COD availability and return policy all appear in a buyer's decision.
3. **Tracking.** The Meta Pixel and the Conversions API tell Meta which people bought, so it can find more of them.
4. **The product page.** A slow or confusing page wastes every click the ad paid for.

## What Google Ads do for an ecommerce store

Google Ads show your ads to people at the moment they search, for example "lawn suits online" or "air fryer price in Pakistan". These people already have intent, so the job is to show up with the right product, price and landing page.

Google Ads campaign types ecommerce stores use most:

- **Search campaigns:** text ads on Google search results, matched to keywords you choose.
- **Shopping campaigns:** product listings with image, price and store name, built from a product feed in Google Merchant Center. Check in Merchant Center which programmes are available for your country and products.
- **Performance Max:** one campaign that runs across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Maps using your products, images and videos.
- **YouTube ads:** video ads for products that need demonstrating.

Google Ads tend to work well for products with clear search demand: electronics, appliances, spare parts, branded products and anything people compare by price.

## Meta Ads vs Google Ads: a quick comparison

| | Meta Ads | Google Ads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How people find you | Your ad appears while they scroll | Your ad appears when they search |
| Best for | Impulse buys, fashion, beauty, new products | Products people already search for |
| What drives results | Creative, offer, audience signals | Keywords, product feed, landing page |
| Key tracking | Meta Pixel and Conversions API | Google Ads conversion tracking and GA4 |
| Typical first campaign | Sales campaign with 3 to 5 creatives | Search or Shopping on your best sellers |

Many stores run both. Meta Ads create demand with discovery and retargeting. Google Ads capture the people searching for your product or brand.

## How to tell if your ads are profitable

Ads Manager and Google Ads both report ROAS, or return on ad spend: revenue divided by ad spend. A 4x ROAS means every Rs 1 of ads brought in Rs 4 of sales. It looks like a win, and it can still lose money.

Here is a hypothetical Rs 4,000 order, with illustrative numbers:

| | Full price | With 20% off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order value | Rs 4,000 | Rs 3,200 |
| Product cost | Rs 1,600 | Rs 1,600 |
| Delivery and COD charges | Rs 400 | Rs 400 |
| Returns and other fees | Rs 560 | Rs 560 |
| Margin before ads | Rs 1,440 (36%) | Rs 640 (20%) |
| Ads at 4x ROAS | Rs 1,000 | Rs 800 |
| **Profit per order** | **Rs 440** | **−Rs 160** |

The ROAS stayed at 4x, and the store went from profit to loss because the discount cut the margin.

The number to know is your **break-even ROAS**:

> Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ margin before ads

At a 36% margin, break-even ROAS is about 2.8x. At 20%, it is 5x. Any campaign below your break-even ROAS loses money on every order, however good it looks in the dashboard.

## How to choose a Meta Ads marketing agency

A Meta Ads marketing agency should be able to talk about your margins as easily as your ads. Use these checks before you sign:

- **Proof in ecommerce.** Ask for campaigns they ran for online stores, with the context: spend, timeframe and what was tracked.
- **Profit-based reporting.** Reports should show orders, cost per order, ROAS against your break-even ROAS, and profit per order.
- **Creative capacity.** Meta results depend on fresh creative. Ask how many new ads they will make each month and who makes them.
- **Tracking setup.** They should set up and test the Meta Pixel, the Conversions API and purchase events before scaling.
- **Account ownership.** Your Business Manager, ad account, pixel and page stay in your business's name.
- **A scaling rule.** Ask what they check before raising your budget. A clear answer covers tracking, cost per order, conversion rate, product economics and stock.

Be careful with guaranteed sales or ROAS figures. No agency can promise a number before it has seen your store, your offer and your costs.

## Questions to ask a Google Ads agency

- Which campaign types would you start with for my products, and why?
- How will you set up conversion tracking, and how will you test it?
- How often will you review search terms and add negative keywords?
- Will you build or fix my product feed in Merchant Center?
- How do you decide between Search campaigns and Performance Max for my store?
- What will you report each month, and how does it connect to my profit?

## Common marketing mistakes that drain ad budgets

1. **Running ads with no conversion tracking,** so the platforms optimise for clicks and the owner guesses at results.
2. **Scaling on ROAS alone,** with no break-even ROAS to compare it to.
3. **Ignoring COD returns.** In Pakistan, returned COD orders cost delivery both ways. Count them in your margin, and cut them with [WhatsApp order confirmation](/blog/whatsapp-automation-business-pakistan) before dispatch.
4. **Sending paid traffic to a slow product page** that loads poorly on mobile data.
5. **Using one creative for months.** Ad fatigue raises costs. Plan new creative every week.
6. **Discounting to fix weak ads.** A discount lowers your margin, which raises your break-even ROAS.
